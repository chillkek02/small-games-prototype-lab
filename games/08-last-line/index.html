How to Play

Keep the city moving. Then evacuate it.

  • ↔
    Draw transit linesSelect a color, then drag from one station to another. Drag from an endpoint to extend it.
  • ○△□
    Shapes are destinationsWaiting people show the station shape they need. They can transfer at shared stations to cross between lines.
  • ↻
    Build loopsDrag between the first and last stop on a line to close or reopen a loop. Loops keep vehicles circulating.
  • ≈
    Budget river crossingsEvery route segment crossing the river uses one bridge. Weekly supplies can add more.
  • ⌫
    Redraw fastUse REMOVE to tap out a stop, or drag between two nonadjacent stops to cut out the section between them.
  • ✚
    Evacuate threatened stationsAmber stations are running out of time. Connect them into any network path that reaches the shelter.
