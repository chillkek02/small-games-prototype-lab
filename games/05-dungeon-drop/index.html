How to play

Choose 1 of 3 triggers → place it → read the fixed entry forecast → drop the adventurer. You no longer aim normal drops. Killing banks bounty; escaping loses it. After each drop refine the board with drafts, rerolls, skips, and removals.

Placement matters: the FOYER favors setup/greed/spawn pieces, the ENGINE favors movement/control/magic, and the KILL FLOOR favors traps and monsters. A ceiling-only kill is deliberately weak.

Tip: each victim builds a TOUR multiplier by reaching deeper rows and activating different triggers. Late tribute demands expect a board-wide engine, not four reliable ceiling traps.
